## Real Life English

### sounds like a broken record (idiom)
- to repeat the same thing over and over again, open in an annoying way.
- My mom sounds like a broken record when she keeps telling me to clean my room
- Every time I call customer service, they sound like a broken record giving me the same response.

### rewind
- to go back , or to make something go back.
- I need to rewind the video to see that part again
- She wished she could rewind time and change the choice she made in past.

### hang up
- to end a phone conversation.
- Don't hang up yet; I still need to tell you what time the movie starts !
- I'm going to hang up now because I'm about to board my flight.
