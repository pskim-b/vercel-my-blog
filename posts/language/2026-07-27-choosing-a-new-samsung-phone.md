## Key Corrections

### 1. Use a gerund after `consider`

**Original**

> I'm considering to buy a new cellular phone.

**More natural**

> I'm considering buying a new phone.

`Consider` is followed by a gerund (`-ing`). In everyday conversation, *phone* is also much more common than *cellular phone*.

### 2. Describe camera performance explicitly

**Original**

> It has 200 megapixel, and it's better.

**More natural**

> It has a 200-megapixel camera, and it performs better.

When discussing smartphone cameras, it sounds more natural to mention the camera explicitly and compare how it performs.

### 3. Use natural language for low-light photography

**Original**

> It strengthens the picture, taking a picture during the night.

**More natural**

> It takes much better photos in low light.

Native speakers usually describe camera quality with expressions such as *takes better photos* or *performs better in low light*.
