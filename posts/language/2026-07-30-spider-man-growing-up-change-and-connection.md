## Reflection

The new Spider-Man movie worked because it stayed true to its action roots while delivering meaningful themes. Rather than simply setting up another installment, it explored what growing up demands: accepting uncertainty, embracing change, and remaining connected to other people.

Peter Parker is no longer just a kid reacting to events around him. His story suggests that maturity means moving forward even when the outcome is unclear. The film also argues that being extraordinary—or feeling different from others—is not a reason to isolate ourselves. Meaningful relationships with people and communities remain essential.

The movie also appeared to set up Spider-Man's eventual return to the larger Marvel universe. This made its themes of change and reconnection feel relevant both to Peter's personal journey and to the direction of the broader story.

## Key Expressions

- **stay true to its core**: preserve the essential identity of something
- **stay true to its action roots**: retain the qualities that originally defined an action series
- **embrace change**: accept change positively rather than resist it
- **stay connected to other people**: maintain meaningful relationships
- **deliver a message**: communicate an idea or theme effectively
- **set up the next movie**: establish events that prepare the story for a sequel

## Key Corrections

### Describing a completed experience

> I just get out after watch the new Spider-Man film.

A natural correction is:

> I just got out after watching the new Spider-Man film.

Use the past tense **got** for a completed event, and use a gerund after **after**.

### Using a modal verb

> Peter Parker should be grow up.

A natural correction is:

> Peter Parker should grow up.

Modal verbs such as **should**, **can**, and **must** are followed by the base form of the verb.

### Talking about change

> We should take our changes.

More natural alternatives are:

> We should embrace change.

> We should accept change.

Here, **change** is used as a general, uncountable idea.

### Talking about relationships

> We should be connected with other people.

A more natural expression is:

> We should stay connected to other people.

### Describing a film's identity

> The movie is true to core.

More natural alternatives are:

> The movie stayed true to its core.

> The movie stayed true to its action roots.

## Next Practice

When discussing a movie, go beyond a general opinion and support it with a specific reason or example.

Instead of:

> The movie was good.

Try:

> I think the movie was good because it balanced action with meaningful themes. For example, Peter Parker's growth reflected the idea that we should embrace change.

This structure makes an opinion clearer, more persuasive, and better suited to advanced conversation.
