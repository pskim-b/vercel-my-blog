# Reconnecting with High School Friends

After several years apart, I reconnected with some high school friends. Seeing them again made me realize how much our lives have changed. We have each built our own families and taken on responsibilities that we did not have as students.

We can no longer spend time together whenever we want or hang out like we did in high school. That realization made the reunion feel both nostalgic and a little sad.

## Natural Expressions

### Talking about reconnecting

Instead of:

> We reconnect after years.

Use:

> We reconnected after several years.

The event already happened, so the verb should be in the past tense.

### Talking about family responsibilities

Instead of:

> We should act as a head of family.

Use:

> We have to take responsibility as the heads of our own families.

Or:

> We have to take responsibility as the heads of our households.

“Head of the family” and “head of the household” are natural expressions. Because the sentence refers to several people and their respective families, the plural form fits the context.

### Talking about spending time together

Instead of:

> We cannot play together like high school students.

Use:

> We can't hang out like we did in high school anymore.

Adults usually use “hang out” rather than “play together” when talking about casually spending time with friends.

### Expressing sadness

Instead of:

> I have sad feeling.

Use:

> I feel a little sad.

Or:

> I have a sad feeling about it.

English commonly expresses emotions with “feel + adjective.”
